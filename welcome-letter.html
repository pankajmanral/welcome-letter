Axis Finance – Code of Conduct

Guidelines to Ethical Conduct & Best Practices

Tele-calling Protocols:

When to Call:

  • Contact prospects only if they have shown interest or consented via approved channels of Axis Finance
  • Calling hours: 0930 Hrs to 1900 Hrs (unless otherwise requested)

Privacy Respect:

  • Discuss the prospect's interest directly with them or with others explicitly authorized by the prospect
  • Customer flagged in "Do Not Disturb" (DND) list should not be contacted

Communication Guidelines:

  • Initial Contact: First contact must be over a call; if unavailable, leave a clear and concise message
  • Follow-Up: Restrict follow-up calls to those who have not explicitly declined further communication
  • Transparency: Clearly identify yourself, your affiliation, and the call's purpose. Misleading statements or misrepresentations are strictly prohibited

Ethics and Integrity:

  • Gifts and Bribes: Strictly prohibited to accept any gifts or bribes
  • Misrepresentation: Avoid false representations about Axis Finance or its products
  • Commitments: Do not make unauthorized commitments on behalf of Axis Finance

Post-Call Etiquette:

  • Provide feedback to Axis Finance on customers' preferences and their 'DND' status
  • Direct any queries about sold products to Customer Service team at Axis Finance

Field Visits Protocol:

  • Maintain appropriate distance
  • Limit to one BDE and one supervisor if necessary
  • Do not enter personal spaces without consent
  • Ensure privacy and limit discussion to business matters only

Appearance and Dress Code:

  • Men: Well-ironed trousers and shirts, preferably with sleeves buttoned down
  • Women: Well-ironed formal attire such as saree or suit, ensuring a well-groomed appearance

Documentation Handling:

  • Ensure all communications are in the format approved by Axis Finance, maintaining professional standards
